Open Roulette is a free, offline roulette simulator with play money only.

<b>Tables</b>
• European (single zero), American (double zero) and French (La Partage / En Prison)
• Complete betting layout: straight, split, street, corner, six line, trio, basket, columns, dozens, red/black, odd/even, high/low
• French call bets: Voisins du Zéro, Tiers du Cylindre, Orphelins, Jeu Zéro

<b>Gameplay</b>
• Realistic wheel and ball animation, cryptographically secure random numbers
• Chip denominations you can customise, undo, clear, rebet and auto-spin
• History, hot/cold numbers and statistics

<b>Design</b>
• Material 3, light / dark / AMOLED themes, four table felts, three chip designs
• Landscape and portrait, TalkBack support, scalable text

<b>Privacy</b>
• No permissions, no network, no ads, no tracking, no Google services
• 100% free software (GPL-3.0-or-later)

This app does not offer real-money gambling.
